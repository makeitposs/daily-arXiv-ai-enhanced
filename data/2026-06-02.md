TL;DR: 研究数据集描述质量对数据集搜索和重用的影响，通过文献框架和大规模消融实验，探讨LLMs生成描述时所需的数据集上下文（标题、模式、代表性数据），评估描述质量并揭示LLMs的稳定描述人格，为LLM支持的数据发布流程提供指导。

Motivation: 数据集搜索和重用受限于元数据质量（如自然语言描述的稀疏或不一致），尽管LLMs可自动生成描述，但缺乏关于“优质描述标准”及“LLMs所需数据集上下文”的实证指导。

Method: 基于文献构建数据集描述质量框架，对252个数据集（1336个CSV文件）进行大规模消融实验：生成LLMs描述的基线场景（标题+模式+代表性数据）、仅标题、标题+模式、标题+模式+代表性数据三种场景，通过LLM-as-a-judge框架和语义描述属性分析（基于质量维度）评估。

Result: 1. 模式单独使用会降低叙事质量（“模式惩罚”）；2. 代表性数据部分恢复基础但不提升人类可读性；3. 不同LLMs呈现稳定描述人格。

Conclusion: 为LLM支持的数据发布流程提供实用指导，明确数据集上下文（标题、模式、代表性数据）对描述质量的影响及LLMs的稳定表现。

Abstract: Dataset search and reuse are strongly constrained by the quality of metadata such as natural language descriptions, which are often sparse or inconsistent. Although large language models (LLMs) can generate such descriptions automatically, little empirical guidance exists on what makes a good dataset description and what dataset context LLMs actually need. We study these questions through a literature-grounded framework of dataset description quality and a large-scale ablation study using 252 datasets (1,336 CSV files) from the European data portal data.europa.eu. We generate descriptions with LLMs in a baseline scenario and two ablation scenarios: (1) using only dataset titles, (2) titles and schema, and (3) titles, schema and representative data, and evaluate them with an LLM-as-a- judge framework and a semantic descriptive attribute analysis grounded in our quality dimensions. Our results reveal a consis- tent schema penalty: table-schemas alone often degrade narrative quality, while representative data partially restores grounding without improving overall human-facing quality. We further show that different LLMs exhibit stable descriptive personas. These findings provide practical guidance for LLM-supported data publishing workflows.
